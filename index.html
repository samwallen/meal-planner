📊 How Voting Works

1. Add recipes to a day → tap 🗳 on a chip to mark it as a Vote Option (turns yellow).

2. Tap 📢 Post Vote to Teams — a card posts to your channel.

3. Members react: 👍 = Option 1   ❤️ = Option 2   😊 = Option 3.

4. Use +/− on the vote tally to record votes as they come in.
